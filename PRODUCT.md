# Product — Industrial Automation Lab

## Purpose

Help a German industrial buyer understand which administrative or service process is being improved, inspect exception behavior and see what remains under human control.

## Audience

Geschäftsführer, technical sales, service, operations, procurement, finance, quality, maintenance, engineering and ERP/IT responsibility in machinery and special-purpose machinery.

## Positioning

Hugo combines approximately eight years of special-purpose machinery and industrial automation context with software and workflow implementation. The lab demonstrates inspectable process preparation—not an AI agency, autonomous engineering or an n8n product.

## Functional promise

Every experiment answers `Problem → Input → Processing → Exception → Human decision → Result`.

The result is a prepared draft with source evidence, audit state and zero external writes. Systems of record remain authoritative.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Deterministic browser workflow and tests.
2. Synthetic fixtures and structured outputs.
3. Sanitized disabled n8n export and manual execution.
4. Screenshots, diagrams and short demo assets.
5. Market-context sources with sample limits.

None of those is customer validation. Observed interest and paid validation remain absent.

## Primary demos

Customer Order Intake, Machine Service Triage and Invoice/PO Matching receive the richest UI and content assets. The ranking is a current implementation hypothesis and can change after real commercial validation.
