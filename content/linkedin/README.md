# German LinkedIn content factory

Everything in this directory is a **local draft**. Nothing was posted, scheduled or sent.

Each workflow directory contains at least three content angles, two complete German posts, hooks, CTAs, a recommended audience, a visual brief and a German exception-state screenshot.

The three polished workflows additionally contain a third post, an eight-slide carousel storyboard, a 30–90 second voice-over script, six reproducible video frames and a 35-second silent MP4.

## Truth rule

Keep `Synthetic Demo`, `Built and testable`, `Mocked adapter` and `Nicht kundenvalidiert` visible where relevant. Do not introduce customers, testimonials, production use, measured savings or ROI without new evidence.
